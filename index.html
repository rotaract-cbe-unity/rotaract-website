About Our Club

Our Story

The Rotaract Club of Coimbatore Unity was chartered on 21st April 2014, under the Family of Rotary Club of Coimbatore East. We are proudly part of Rotary International District 3206 (Coimbatore | Pallakkad).

With Club ID 91594, we have been serving our community for over a decade, impacting thousands of lives through meaningful service projects.
